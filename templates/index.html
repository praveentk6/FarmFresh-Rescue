How FarmFresh Rescue Solves Spoilage

Bridging the critical gap between farmers suffering post-harvest bottlenecks and commercial buyers looking for quality produce at direct farm-gate rates.

👨‍🌾

1. Farmers Post Harvest

Farmers enter produce quantity, asking price, harvest date, village, and photos. If a batch remains unsold after 3 days, the system automatically marks it with a high-priority Needs Attention badge.

🏪

2. Nearby Buyers Discover

Hotels, restaurants, juice stalls, and retail stores search by crop, district, or PIN code. They can prioritize older batches to secure huge wholesale savings while saving food from rotting.

🤝

3. Direct Connect & Rescue

Buyers send direct enquiries, call the farmer immediately, or message on WhatsApp. Produce is collected or delivered rapidly, and the farmer marks the listing as Sold.
