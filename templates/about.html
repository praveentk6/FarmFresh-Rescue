Mission & Architecture

Rescuing Agricultural Produce.
Empowering Tamil Nadu Farmers.

FarmFresh Rescue is a real-time digital agricultural waste prevention platform engineered to connect farmers holding unsold surplus fruits, vegetables, and grains directly with local commercial buyers before spoilage occurs.

The Agricultural Waste Challenge

In India, an estimated 16% to 30% of perishable agricultural produce (notably tomatoes, leafy vegetables, onions, and seasonal fruits) is lost post-harvest due to market access bottlenecks, temporary wholesale gluts, and lack of immediate cold storage facilities.

During peak seasons in Tamil Nadu regions like Ottanchathiram, Rayakottai, and Pollachi, farmers are often compelled to distress-sell or abandon healthy harvests on roadside verges when central mandis face oversupply. FarmFresh Rescue converts this unsold inventory into discovery opportunities for nearby hotels, restaurants, caterers, and food processing units.

Needs Attention Badge

How Urgency is Tracked & Flagged

When a farmer posts a produce listing, our system monitors its duration on the platform. If a listing remains unsold and available for 3 or more days, the application automatically flags it with the high-visibility Needs Attention badge.

⚠️ Transparency Notice & Disclaimer:
The "Needs Attention" badge indicates listing age only (that 3+ days have elapsed since listing without a recorded sale). It serves to prioritize clearance and alerts conscious buyers to intervene before crops rot. The badge does not claim to perform laboratory freshness verification, microbiological testing, or guarantee commercial performance. Buyers and farmers are encouraged to inspect produce upon collection.

Key Agricultural Hubs in Tamil Nadu

  • Krishnagiri & Dharmapuri: Country hybrid tomatoes, mangoes, and capsicum.
  • Dindigul (Ottanchathiram): Famous Bellary small onions (shallots), drumsticks, and green chilies.
  • The Nilgiris (Ooty & Ketti): High-altitude crunchy mountain carrots, cabbage, and potatoes.
  • Pollachi & Anamalai: Premium tender coconuts (elaneer) and organic bananas.
  • Madurai: Fragrant Madurai Malli (jasmine buds) requiring immediate daily clearance.
  • Thanjavur & Kaveri Delta: Ponni paddy, black gram, and green gram harvests.
